Why Do Commercial Facilities Need Custom Networks?

No two commercial spaces are identical. Concrete walls, steel beams, metal racking, and high client density affect how Wi-Fi signals travel. Standard ISP business modems fail under heavy multi-user loads.

POS Bandwidth Priority

Quality of Service (QoS) rules prioritizing payment terminals and financial transactions over guest streaming and staff web browsing.

Guest vs. Corporate Isolation

Isolated guest Wi-Fi networks with customizable captive portals, preventing visitor access to internal servers and cameras.

10 Gbps Fiber Backbones

Multi-gigabit optical fiber inter-building links connecting main server rooms to remote warehouse wings and outdoor loading bays.
