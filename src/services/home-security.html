Intelligent Defense

Smart Home Security Automation

Your security system doesn't just react to break-ins — it actively deters intruders by working in sync with your lighting, shading, and power systems.

Automated Security Lighting

If perimeter cameras or motion sensors detect unusual night activity, exterior landscape lights illuminate at 100% brightness and interior lights flood the hallways to deter unwanted visitors.

Motorized Shade Occupancy Simulation

When away on vacation, your motorized window shades open and close on randomized schedules alongside interior lights to create a realistic "lived-in" appearance.

24/7 Cellular Backup Protection

Even if an intruder cuts your internet lines or a storm knocks out utility power, dedicated cellular backup transmitters and battery units ensure emergency signals reach monitoring dispatchers immediately.

Environmental Water & Fire Safety

Wireless flood sensors near sump pumps and water heaters automatically trigger main water shut-off valves upon detecting leaks, preventing costly basement flooding.
