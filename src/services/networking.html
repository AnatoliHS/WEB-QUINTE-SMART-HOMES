Residential Cabling

Wired Stability Meets Outdoor Wireless Freedom

We run high-speed hardwired Cat6a Ethernet cables to stationary high-bandwidth devices — like 4K Smart TVs, gaming consoles, Apple TVs, security cameras, and media servers.

Then, we place discreet ceiling-mounted and weather-sealed outdoor access points so your laptops, tablets, and phones stay connected seamlessly anywhere on your property.

Pre-wiring during new home builds or renovations ensures your system is future-proof and ready for simple upgrades as family technology evolves.

Residential Pre-Wiring Includes:

  • Cat6a gigabit cabling for 4K TVs, cameras & gaming
  • Structured cabling for Cable/Satellite TV distribution
  • Starlink satellite dish roof & chimney mounting integration
  • Indoor and all-weather outdoor architectural audio wiring
  • Isolated guest access & IoT smart device VLAN security
