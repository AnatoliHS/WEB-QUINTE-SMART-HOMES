What is Starlink Satellite Internet?

Starlink utilizes low-Earth orbit (LEO) satellites to deliver high-speed, low-latency internet to locations where ground fiber or cable infrastructure is unavailable. With unlimited data and speeds up to 250+ Mbps, Starlink is the premier solution for rural living and remote business operations.

Important Information

Essential Setup & Client Guidelines

To ensure a seamless installation day, please review these key purchasing and site requirements.

1. Ordering Your Starlink Equipment

Quinte Smart Homes provides expert setup and installation, but clients must order their Starlink kit directly from Starlink.com due to third-party seller restrictions. Contact us as soon as you order so we can reserve your installation date upon equipment delivery.

2. Official Starlink Mount Selection

While we frequently stock popular wall and ridge mounts, we strongly recommend purchasing your mount directly when buying your Starlink kit. If you require a mount from our stock, please notify us in advance so we can verify inventory and pricing.

3. Line-of-Sight & Roof Peak Clearance

Starlink dishes require a clear, unobstructed view of the sky to communicate with passing satellites. Trees or nearby structures blocking the view will degrade performance. We install dishes on roof peaks, chimneys, or dedicated poles to maximize sky visibility.

4. Professional Cable Pass-Throughs

Starlink installations require drilling a minimum 3/4-inch exterior hole. Our licensed electricians execute clean, weather-sealed wall penetrations through wood, brick, masonry, or stucco, neatly running cables through floors and baseboards directly to your utility rack.
