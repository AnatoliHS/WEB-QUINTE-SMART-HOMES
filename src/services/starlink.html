Line-of-Sight & Sky Clearance

"Starlink dishes require a clear, unobstructed 100-degree view of the sky. Nearby mature trees or roof eaves blocking dish sightlines will degrade speed."

We mount dishes on roof peaks, chimneys, or dedicated 20ft mast poles to overcome tree canopy obstacles.
