Kanata

Kanata is Ottawa's premier western tech-hub, featuring high demand for family-friendly townhomes and new construction detached houses.

Barrhaven

Barrhaven is a rapidly expanding suburban district in southwest Ottawa known for highly-rated public schools, retail amenities, and affordable single-family homes.

Orléans

Located in Ottawa East, Orléans offers beautiful Ottawa River waterfront properties, rapid transit access, and a strong bilingual community.

Nepean

Nepean is a highly established western suburb featuring a mix of mid-century homes on large lots and modern infill developments.

Ottawa South encompasses vibrant urban neighborhoods like The Glebe and Old Ottawa South, characterized by historic properties and walkability.
