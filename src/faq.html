Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about navigating the Ottawa Real Estate market.

How much does it cost to use a Realtor to buy a house in Ottawa?

For buyers in Ottawa, using a Realtor is typically free. Real estate commissions are almost universally structured so that the seller covers the cost of both agents out of the final sale price. You receive elite market knowledge, negotiation leverage, and legal protection at zero direct cost to you.

How long does it take to buy a house in Ottawa?

The timeline varies based on market conditions and your criteria. Typically, house hunting takes between 3 to 8 weeks. Once a firm offer is negotiated and accepted, the standard closing period (the time it takes to arrange financing and legal transfers) generally takes 30 to 90 days. We work entirely at your speed.

What are the best neighborhoods for families in Ottawa?

Ottawa is known globally for its high quality of living and phenomenal suburban districts. Highly recommended areas include:

  • Kanata: Perfect for tech professionals, excellent public schools, and massive green spaces.
  • Barrhaven: Rapidly expanding, incredibly affordable townhouses, and diverse community centers.
  • Orleans: Beautiful bilingual heritage along the Ottawa river, featuring brand-new LRT transit extensions.
Do I need to be pre-approved for a mortgage before house hunting?

Yes, obtaining a mortgage pre-approval is the absolute first step you should take. It locks in your interest rate for 90-120 days, determines your absolute maximum budget, and shows sellers that you are a serious, qualified buyer—which is critical during intense negotiations.
